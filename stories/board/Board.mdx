# The bakesbymom board

Every token, component and section in this system comes from the 16 pins on the [nuttychip / bakesbymom](https://in.pinterest.com/nuttychip/bakesbymom/) Pinterest board. Each pin has its own analysis (layout, sections top to bottom, components, measured colors and type, and what Bakes by Mom takes or leaves). Click a title to open it.

**Cherry tags** mark the sections a pin leads; **blue tags** mark alternates and borrowed pieces. The full index is [`design/board/README.md`](https://github.com/Tamanna-57/Bakesbymom_/blob/main/design/board/README.md).

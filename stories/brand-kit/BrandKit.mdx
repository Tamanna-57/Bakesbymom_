# Brand kit

Everything here comes from the 16 pins on the bakesbymom board. The written version, with every rule linked to its pins, is [`design/board/brand-kit.md`](https://github.com/Tamanna-57/Bakesbymom_/blob/main/design/board/brand-kit.md).

## The idea

> **"Mom's recipe notebook, open on the counter of a little pink-and-blue bake shop."**

Three words to check every design against: **warm, handmade, clear.** The board keeps returning to handmade details (doodles, handwriting, tape, stickers), nostalgic paper (tickets, stamps, seals, polaroids), pastels held together by cocoa outlines and cherry accents, and a founder who signs her name.

Clear space is the height of the "B" all round. Minimum size: horizontal 120px wide, stacked 64px. Cherry on canvas, beige, pink or blue; canvas on cocoa or cherry. Don't stretch, outline or recolor it, or swap the script.

## Voice

Mom talks like Mom: warm, plain, a little funny, never salesy.

| Say                                             | Not                                                     |
| ----------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Baked this morning."                           | "Artisanal, freshly crafted daily."                     |
| "Order by Wednesday for Saturday."              | "Orders subject to availability windows."               |
| "Most bakes can be made gluten-free. Just ask." | "Dietary accommodations may be available upon request." |
| "Sold out this week, back next Saturday."       | "Out of stock."                                         |
| "with love, Mom"                                | "Best regards, the Bakes by Mom team"                   |

## How we sound

| We are               | So we write                                      | Not                                 |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------------------- |
| **Warm**             | "Baked fresh this morning, still a little warm." | "Products are prepared daily."      |
| **Clear**            | "Order by Wednesday for Saturday pick-up."       | "Lead times may vary."              |
| **Honest**           | "Made in a home kitchen that also handles nuts." | Hiding allergens in the small print |
| **A little playful** | "Impossible to eat just one slice."              | Puns in every sentence              |

- **Buttons say what happens:** "Add to box", "Order a cake", "See this week's menu". Avoid "Submit" and "Click here".
- **Prices are plain:** "From $38" for customizable items, "$14 / dozen" for sets. Use DM Mono and tabular figures.
- **Mom signs things.** The "Meet Mom" section, order confirmations and thank-you cards end with a script signature: _with love, Mom_.

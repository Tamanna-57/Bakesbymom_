# Colors

Measured from the 16 pins on the bakesbymom board, and nothing else. Five core colors (**baby blue, baby pink, beige, cherry red and cocoa brown**) plus two small helpers (butter and matcha). Steps marked ★ were measured directly from a pin; the rest are interpolated in OKLCH between those anchors. The full write-up is [`design/board/palette.md`](https://github.com/Tamanna-57/Bakesbymom_/blob/main/design/board/palette.md).

**Components never use primitives directly.** They use the semantic layer (`bg`, `text`, `border`, `action`, `status`, `tone`). Every pairing is checked against WCAG AA by `packages/tokens/test/tokens.test.mjs`.

## Tones

Tones are how Badge, Card, StepList, Accordion, PriceTag, Seal, Marquee and the patterns get their color. Each has a soft pairing (`fg` on `bg`) and a filled pairing (`on-solid` on `solid`), and both are AA.

<ToneGrid />

## Rules of thumb

- **About 60% beige, 25% blue and pink, 10% cocoa, 5% cherry.** Cream does most of the work.
- **Bright cherry once per page.** `bg.brand` is for one bold band (the weekly schedule). Buttons use deep cherry (`action.primary`).
- **Pastels are grounds, not text.** Put cocoa text on blue, pink and beige.
- **Don't stack blue and pink sections back to back** without a beige band or an edge between them.
- **Focus is navy on purpose.** `border.focus` (`blue-700`) never gets confused with cherry.
- **Left out on purpose:** the orange of pin 07, the green and peach of 06 and the lavender of 15. Their layouts are used, recolored.

# Spacing & Shape

## Spacing

A 4px grid. Token names are multiples of 4: `space-6` is 24px and `space-16` is 64px. In Tailwind v4 the same grid is `--spacing: 0.25rem` (`p-6` = 24px).

## Radius

Two shape families: **pills** for anything you can tap (buttons, inputs, badges, chips) and **soft corners** for surfaces (14–28px cards). Photos can use the arch (`--bbm-radius-arch`), oval or circle frames.

<RadiusScale />

## Borders

Outlines are the structure of the system. The 1.5px cocoa _sticker_ outline makes cards, buttons and photos look cut from paper.

<BorderScale />

## Elevation

Sticker shadows are hard offsets with no blur, so elements look stuck onto the page rather than floating above it. Use `sm` for buttons and markers, `md` for cards and `lg` for hero moments. The soft `photo` shadow is only for polaroids and cut-out photos; `overlay` is only for menus and popovers.

<ShadowScale />

## Motion

Small, warm and optional. Buttons press in with `bounce`; everything else uses `standard`. Marquees and spinning seals stop entirely under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

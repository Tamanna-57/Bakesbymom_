# Typography

Five families, each with one job, all read from the board. All are free Google Fonts (SIL OFL), loaded by `@bakesbymom/ui/fonts.css`.

| Family              | Job                                                     | From the board                                                               |
| ------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Fraunces**        | Headlines, prices, the logo                             | The soft serif display of pins 02, 05, 13 and 15; the soft black serif of 11 |
| **DM Sans**         | Body, UI, labels; 800–900 for one heavy title           | The clean sans bodies everywhere; the heavy grotesque of 16                  |
| **Pinyon Script**   | One accent word, "by Mom", signatures (32px and up)     | The formal script of 16 ("Fillings") and 10                                  |
| **Caveat**          | Handwritten notes, captions, pill labels (20px and up)  | The handwriting on 15 ("catch me if you candy!"), 04, 13 and 01              |
| **Cherry Bomb One** | One bubble word per page, always outlined (64px and up) | GALLERY on 01, the pink marquee on 11                                        |

## Type scale

Display sizes are **fluid**: they scale between the phone and desktop sizes with `clamp()`, following the smaller of the screen's width and height, so a short laptop screen gets a smaller headline. The largest size is never more than twice the smallest and every bound is in `rem`, so text still doubles with browser zoom (WCAG 1.4.4). Use the classes (`.bbm-text-display-xl`) or the variables (`--bbm-text-display-xl-size`, `-line-height`, `-tracking`, `-weight`, `-family`).

1. **A serif headline with one word in script**, as on pins 13 and 15. In components, wrap the word in `<em>`.
2. **A hand-drawn underline** under one word, as on pin 03 (`.bbm-squiggle`).
3. **A handwritten aside**, tilted, next to the product, as on pin 15.
4. **One bubble word** per page, outlined with a hard cocoa shadow, as on pin 01.

## Rules

- Body copy is 17px minimum on the web. Ingredients and small print can go to 15px.
- Script never sets more than three words, and never below 32px.
- Handwriting is for asides. Anything a customer needs to know (dates, prices, allergens) is in DM Sans.
- Labels and eyebrows are uppercase with tracking; buttons are sentence case and bold.
